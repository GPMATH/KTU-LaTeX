\section{Direct prior art and industrial inspection systems}
\subsection{Direct academic research on coated PCBs}
The two 2021 Korean Journal of Broadcast Engineering papers by Lee et al. directly address conformal-coated PCB bubble inspection \citep{lee2021cnn,lee2021transfer}. In the earlier approach, UV images were processed to find dark-centre candidates, after which CNN-based verification rejected non-bubble lookalikes. The authors' abstract reports precision 0.805, recall 0.763 and F1 0.767. This discrepancy is precisely why the original evidence matters. In the later paper, VGG-style CNN transfer learning is reported to improve bubble detection to F1 0.9044.

Zouhri and colleagues presented a Faster R-CNN assessment of coating bubbles at ICM 2021 \citep{zouhri2021faster}.

In 2024, Choi et al. addressed UV irradiance selection for small conformal-coating bubbles, using image-level colour/brightness descriptors, feature-space distances, YOLOv8 and image tiling \citep{choi2024uv}. Wang et al. reported a YOLOv8-Nano variant with attention and feature fusion for PCB coating bubble detection, with a 12\% relative performance improvement for smaller bubbles in the institutional abstract \citep{wang2025bubble}.

Patel et al. presented a customised Faster R-CNN with ResNet-152 and enhanced proposal mechanisms for capillary and dewetting patterns on conformal-coated PCBAs \citep{patel2026coverage}. The abstract reports recall of 98.53\% and 95.49\% for two defect categories, respectively.

\subsection{Relevant work}
General PCB AOI surveys discuss sensor/illumination choices, geometric comparison, segmentation, classification and manufacturing integration \citep{ebayyeh2020aoi,zhou2023pcb,ieee2023pcb}. The 1988 Hara et al. system exploited ultraviolet-excited \emph{PCB base-material fluorescence} to distinguish copper patterns \citep{hara1988fluorescent}.

Outside electronics, Han et al. used autoencoders, self-labelling, and convolutional models to identify coating boundaries on fuel-injection nozzles \citep{han2025coating}. Their fully accessible paper reports over 95\% interface localization within $\pm 6$ pixels and 7.18 images per second.

\subsection{Synthesis}
The reviewed commercial pages demonstrate use of reference plans, but do not independently establish how much they improve each specific defect class.
