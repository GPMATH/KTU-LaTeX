\section{Algorithms, public datasets and methodological trade-offs}
\subsection{Classical vision is a necessary baseline}
For a single known PCB design under controlled UV illumination, classical vision offers a strong, interpretable starting point. Correct dark frames, gain and spatial irradiation; align to fiducials or stable geometric features. Otsu's histogram thresholding is a principled unsupervised baseline \citep{otsu1979}. Geometric alignment can use fiducials and a planar projective transformation with robust RANSAC fitting \citep{fischler1981ransac}; camera intrinsics and lens distortion can be estimated with planar calibration \citep{zhang2000calibration}. Image registration reviews distinguish feature-based from intensity-based matching and the risks from resampling/occlusion \citep{zitova2003registration}.

\subsection{CNN and transformer segmentation}
U-Net remains a reasonable low-data segmentation comparator because its skip connections support preservation of spatial detail \citep{ronneberger2015unet}. DeepLabv3+ uses atrous processing and a boundary-refining decoder, offering a more complex reference model \citep{chen2018deeplab}. Lightweight backbones such as MobileNetV3 may reduce CPU cost \citep{howard2019mobile}.

Swin Transformer aggregates hierarchical contextual representations \citep{liu2021swin}; SegFormer pairs multi-scale features with a small MLP decoder \citep{xie2021segformer}; Mask2Former supports multiple mask segmentation tasks \citep{cheng2022mask2former}; MobileViT investigates low-resource global representation \citep{mehta2022mobilevit}. The real evaluation criterion here is not a published score on ADE20K. A practical progression is (1) a classical reference-aware baseline; (2) small U-Net; (3) SegFormer-B0 or MobileNetV3-based segmentation if justified.

For very small bubbles, downsampling a full 4000-pixel image to 512 or 640 pixels wide changes effective sampling from 20 to 125--156 $\mu$m/px. Consequently, a nominal 0.2~mm defect that spans ten original pixels may span only about 1.3--1.6 pixels after aggressive resize. The Choi et al. paper is direct evidence that tiling has been investigated for tiny coating bubbles \citep{choi2024uv}.

\subsection{Anomaly detection and scarce defects}
PatchCore represents acceptable image patches with a feature-memory bank \citep{roth2022patchcore}; DRAEM trains with synthetically generated anomalies \citep{zavrtanik2021draem}; EfficientAD explores a low-latency student--teacher arrangement and global context \citep{batzner2024efficientad}.

The uncertainty-aware PCB method $\mathrm{U}^{2}\mathrm{D}^{2}$PCB illustrates reconstruction and discriminative anomaly logic on conventional PCB data \citep{u2d2pcb2024}. Likewise, CM-UNetv2 targets small ordinary PCB features and boundaries, offering a hypothesis about feature preservation rather than evidence of coating behavior \citep{guo2025cmunet}.

DsPCBSD+ provides explicitly downloadable images and object annotations under an official Figshare record \citep{dspcbfigshare}. This makes it useful for validating image loaders, annotation conversion and neural training code, but those image labels refer to bare PCB surface/copper defects. DeepPCB has reference/test image pairs and defect boxes, which could help test registration software before any UV data are captured \citep{tang2019deeppcb}. MVTec AD and LOCO AD carry non-commercial dataset licences; they must not silently migrate into a factory training or redistribution workflow \citep{mvtecdataset,bergmann2022loco}. VisA lists a more permissive CC BY dataset licence but is still not a substitute for collecting UV coating images \citep{visa2022dataset}.

\subsection{Dataset acquisition is a central experimental contribution}
A purpose-built UV coating dataset is therefore a \emph{necessary deliverable} and possibly an independent scholarly contribution. It should include nominal 70~mm boards across physical board IDs, coating lots, component configurations and controlled acquisition conditions; carefully separated genuine defects and induced test coupons.
