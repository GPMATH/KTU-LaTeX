\section{Conclusion}

This report provided a comparison of three model variants on a specific dataset
containing coffee leaf disease images. After the additional experiment, the best result
was obtained by the MobileNetV2 model with the dense classifier head. The original
MobileNetV2 baseline achieved 94.23\% test accuracy in the repeated comparison, while
the dense-head MobileNetV2 reached 97.53\%. This showed that the lower performance of
the first MobileNetV2 model was likely caused partly by the very small classifier head
rather than by the MobileNetV2 feature extractor itself. The Simple CNN still remains an
important comparison because it achieved strong accuracy when trained from scratch, but
the improved MobileNetV2 model became the best-performing model in the final experiment.

All model variants were used for the comparison. On the other hand, some limitations
were also taken into account. Thus, among the technical errors, there were the use of
incorrect MobileNetV2 preprocessing, lack of evaluation of the fine-tuned MobileNetV2
model separately, increased overfitting risk in the dense-head model, and possibility of
data leakage due to image augmentations.
